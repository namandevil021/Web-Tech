A.P.J Abdul Kalam Community

Our community is a renowned not-for-profit organization in our Nation that fights hunger by implementing the mid-day meal scheme. Our goal is to eliminate hunger, counter malnutrition. We operate state-of-the-art centralized kitchens and partners with government bodies to cook and deliver hygienic, hot meals who needed.

Why You Should Donate Food

Donating food is important because it fights hunger, reduces waste, and helps the planet. It provides daily meals to people in need, keeps safe food out of garbage dumps, and builds a stronger community.
